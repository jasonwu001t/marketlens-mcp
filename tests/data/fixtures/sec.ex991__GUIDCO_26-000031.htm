Guidco Reports Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results

SANTA CLARA, Calif., Aug. 4, 2026 -- Guidco Synthetic Inc today announced revenue for the second quarter of 2026 of $11.5 billion, gross margin of 54%, operating income of $2.0 billion, net income of $2.3 billion and diluted earnings per share of $1.38.

Second Quarter 2026 Highlights

  • Revenue was $11.5 billion, up 50% year-over-year.
  • Data Center segment revenue was a record $6.7 billion.
  • “We expect Data Center sales to accelerate in the second half of 2026,” said the chief financial officer.

Current Outlook

Guidco’s outlook statements are based on current expectations. The following statements are forward-looking and actual results could differ materially.

For the third quarter of 2026, Guidco expects revenue to be approximately $13 billion, plus or minus $300 million. The mid-point of the revenue range represents year-over-year growth of approximately 41% and a sequential increase of approximately 13%. Non-GAAP gross margin is expected to be approximately 56%.

Guidco Teleconference

Guidco will hold a conference call at 2:00 p.m. PT today. We expect strong demand to continue through the year.
