Testco Synthetic Corp Hosts Investor Day 2026

SAN JOSE, Calif., May 12, 2026 -- Testco Synthetic Corp today hosted its Investor Day 2026 and outlined its long-term financial model.

Over the next three to five years, Testco expects revenue growth greater than 20% CAGR.

The company targets non-GAAP operating margin of approximately 30% by 2029.

These statements are forward-looking.
