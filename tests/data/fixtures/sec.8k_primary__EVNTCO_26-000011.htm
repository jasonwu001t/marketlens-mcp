Eventco Synthetic Inc

Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

On January 6, 2026, Eventco Synthetic Inc (the “Company”) entered into a Supply Agreement with Northwind Compute LLC under which the Company will supply accelerators for Northwind’s data centers over five years. A copy of the Agreement is filed as Exhibit 10.1 hereto.

Item 3.02 Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities.

In connection with the Agreement, the Company issued Northwind a warrant to purchase up to 12,000,000 shares of common stock at $0.01 per share, vesting in tranches tied to deliveries, in a private placement exempt under Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act.
